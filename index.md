# Fruitopia — Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Fruitopia is a learning game for children aged 2–5. We built it so that it
**does not collect personal information from anyone**.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising of any kind, and no advertising ID.
- No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No accounts, sign-ups, chat, or social features.
- No location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
- We never sell or share data — there is nothing to share.

## What stays on your device

When a grown-up creates a child profile, the app stores the nickname, age,
chosen avatar, game progress, stickers, and play time **only on this device**,
in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded. Deleting a profile in the
Parents' Area, or uninstalling the app, removes it permanently.

## Purchases

If a grown-up buys the one-time "unlock everything" option, the payment is
handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. Google Play
tells the app only whether the unlock was bought, so it can be restored on
your other devices that use the same Google account.

## Internet

The app works fully offline. It only contacts Google Play to check for, make
or restore a purchase, and only when a grown-up opens the Parents' Area or the
app starts (to keep an existing unlock in sync).

## Grown-up areas

The Parents' Area, purchases, and any link that leaves the app (such as
"Contact support", which opens your email app) are protected by a parent gate
that young children cannot solve.

## Compliance

We designed Fruitopia to follow the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S.
Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR rules for
children (GDPR-K).
